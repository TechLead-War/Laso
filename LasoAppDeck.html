Privacy and data control are the emotional payoff of a settings screen here, yet nothing at the top makes the user feel in command of their own data the moment they land.

The three metric chips floating on the orb are small and easy to miss, the single number sits static with no sense of trajectory or "what closes the gap" until you scroll, and there is no forward-looking hook tying today's number to a future self the user cares about.
