Laso · Onboarding Rethink · 6 Motion Variants per Screen

First principles: every screen questioned, less text, real motion (it plays live). Six thoughts each: Curiosity, Personal, Social Proof, Future Self, Show-dont-tell, Cut/Merge. Blue ring = pick. Red phone = recommend cutting.

My rethink (read this first)

Honest first-principles verdict: the current funnel is 17 screens (2 locked welcome + 15 in the funnel file) and it is too long in the wrong places and too short on the one thing that matters. It spends 5 screens collecting answers (About, Goal, Symptoms, Prediction, Activity) and then commits the cardinal sin: it SPLITS the payoff across four separate "finding" screens (Verdict, Heart, Sleep, HRV pattern) that each re-animate a single fact. That is four weak peaks instead of one strong one, which is exactly what peak-end memory punishes, and it buries the actual Vitality Age number deep in the flow while motivation is bleeding out (RevenueCat: motivation is highest at install and drops with every screen before the offer). The leaner flow is roughly 7 strong screens, each carrying ONE decision or ONE feeling: (1) Orb welcome [locked], (2) Passport vs heart [locked], (3) Personal calibration in ONE screen (age + sex + one goal, merged, motion = number building), (4) "Your watch already knows your heart" -> Health permission folded in (one screen, pulseRings, the consent is the action not a separate privacy billboard), (5) Live scan -> Vitality Age count-up REVEAL (the single peak: count-up + one 400ms success haptic + soft tone landing on the same frame, then the number rests still), (6) Future-self commitment (the END high note: a wordless older-vs-younger trajectory of THEIR own number, plus a one-line message to their future self the app returns in one year), (7) Paywall that reflects the number back, short-form, one toggle, no fresh benefits list. This is not minimalism for its own sake. The research is explicit that fewer screens does not automatically convert better (Adapty lost 13% shortening a working flow). The cuts target screens that do NO work, and the keeps are the screens that personalize or earn a micro-commitment, because those are the ones that lift conversion. The whole pre-paywall flow should be answerable in about a minute, near-zero reading, with the real computed Vitality Age delivered BEFORE the price appears so the user has already felt the payoff.

Screens to cut or merge

  • CUT Symptoms (screen 5): a checklist of what feels wrong reads as a symptom inventory to an already-anxious 35-50 heart cohort, adds cognitive load, and does no work the watch data cannot do better. Delete it.
  • CUT HRV pattern (screen 14): it is the third re-animation of a single body fact and a fourth weak peak. The HRV science is real but parameter-specific and slow (8+ weeks, modest gain), so a standalone HRV screen over-promises and over-explains. Fold any HRV signal into the one reveal.
  • CUT Heart (screen 12) and Sleep (screen 13) as standalone screens: merge their one-fact insights INTO the reveal as supporting detail beneath the Vitality Age number, not as separate full screens that each re-animate. One peak, supported, not four peaks competing.
  • MERGE Prediction (screen 6) into the reveal: a separate 'we predict short nights are linked' screen is a promise the reveal should just KEEP. Do not pre-announce the insight on its own screen, deliver it once.
  • MERGE Activity (screen 7) into calibration or drop it: if the watch already sends activity to Apple Health, asking the user to self-rate their weekly effort is redundant data the scan reads directly. Keep only if the funnel proves the watch data is too sparse to trust.
  • MERGE About (3) + Goal (4) into ONE personal-calibration screen: age, sex, and one goal in a single low-load screen where motion shows the number starting to build. Two form screens become one.
  • MERGE Health permission (screen 9) into the wearable screen (8): the permission grant IS the action of 'your watch already knows your heart, let it speak.' A standalone permission screen is a motivation tax; never let it become a negative peak with a red denial path, fail soft.
  • CUT Sign in (screen 16) as a standalone gate before the paywall: defer auth to after the user commits, or fold it silently into the paywall. A login wall between the future-self high note and the price is an accidental negative peak.
  • Net: 15 funnel screens collapse to roughly 5 (calibration, wearable+permission, scan+reveal, future-self, paywall), for ~7 total with the two locked welcome screens.

New mechanics worth adding

  • LIVE COUNT-UP REVEAL as the single peak: tap to scan -> the app visibly pulls the user's real beats/HRV from their own Apple Watch data -> the Vitality Age number counts up and LOCKS. This is the Superhuman move (felt, not told): the number must feel earned from their body in real time, never pre-baked. This is the one justified longer animation (1.5-2.5s) because the user is watching it on purpose.
  • FUTURE-SELF TRAJECTORY (wordless): on the pure-black field, two glowing lines from THEIR own number, one drifting older (do nothing), one bending younger (act now). Hershfield's strongest evidence: seeing your own future trajectory cuts future-discounting. It must be the user's own number animating, never a stock face or invented person.
  • MESSAGE TO YOUR FUTURE SELF returned in one year: a one-line note the user types or confirms, scheduled to surface on the same calendar day next year. This is a textbook active written commitment (Cialdini) plus an implementation intention plus the END high note (peak-end). Keep the ask one-tap-easy and place it right before pricing so the remembered close is hope and accountability, not a sales ask.
  • ONE matched social-proof line, FLAGGED AS NEEDS REAL DATA, folded into the reveal not a separate screen: e.g. 'Many people who started where you are raised their HRV over a few months.' Must be softened ('many people', over months), goal-matched, and backed by real Laso cohort data before shipping. The HRV magnitude (~15-20% RMSSD over 3-6 months, DREW/PLoS One 2008) is citable, but never a hard fast promise, and never a fake live ticker of people 'checking right now', which is a banned dark pattern.
  • PEAK HAPTIC + TONE on the reveal frame: a single ~400ms success haptic and a soft tone landing on the SAME frame the number locks (JCR 2026: a 400ms vibration is a real brain-level reward, but only if scarce and 1:1 with the meaningful event). Reuse Apple's documented success haptic so it carries built-in meaning. Fire it exactly once, nowhere else in the flow.

Boldest moves

  • Collapse the four 'finding' screens (Verdict, Heart, Sleep, HRV) into ONE reveal. This is the single highest-leverage move: stop spreading the aha across four weak animated peaks and concentrate the entire delight budget (sight + 400ms haptic + tone, same frame) on the one moment the Vitality Age number locks. Peak-end memory ignores the average of the middle, so a lean middle is fine but four competing peaks dilute the only one that matters.
  • Deliver the REAL computed Vitality Age from the user's own watch BEFORE the paywall, live and earned, not pre-baked. Beat Headspace: hand over the actual value before asking for money so the price lands on a user who has already felt the payoff. The live computation from their own HealthKit data IS the proof and the aha.
  • Make the ENDING a future-self commitment, not a price wall. A one-line message the app returns in exactly one year, over a wordless trajectory of their own number bending younger. This is the high-note close peak-end demands, it is a real commitment device (Cialdini + Hershfield), and it needs zero invented stats to work. The price appears AFTER this, so the remembered ending is hope, not a sales ask.
  • Kill the symptom checklist entirely. For an anxious heart-conscious 35-50 user, asking 'which of these wrong things feel like you' primes fear and reads as a diagnosis intake. The watch data carries this signal honestly; the self-report only adds dread and load.
  • Cut copy to one line or none on every screen and let the escalating motion arc carry meaning, then audit ruthlessly: if an animation is not delivering a specific fact (the age, the change, the direction of travel), remove it; if a screen has no input and no payoff, delete the screen. The biggest repeatable conversion win in the whole research set is removing TEXT and redundant choices, not adding screens, applied straight through to a short-form paywall that restates the ONE number instead of listing benefits.
  • Flag every social-proof number as NEEDS REAL DATA and ship ZERO of them until backed by Laso's own cohort analytics. For this anxious audience a number that reads as fake poisons trust in the Vitality Age itself, which is worse than showing nothing. Specific, true, and goal-matched, or omit it.

Motion strategy

"One escalating motion arc across the whole flow, not the same flourish repeated per screen (that reads cheap and irritating by screen three for a premium brand). The arc is: orb pulsing in time with a heartbeat (welcome) -> passport-to-heart morph (concept) -> number building as goals/age feed it (calibration) -> pulse rings on the wrist (wearable) -> the SCAN reading real data -> the count-up REVEAL (the peak) -> the slow future-self trajectory (the end). Each beat is cause-and-effect, not ambient sparkle. Hard rules from the research: (1) Every animation must pass a one-job test (feedback, state change, navigation metaphor, or demonstrating the watch-data-to-age mechanism). If you cannot name what it teaches or confirms, cut it. (2) Keep transitions FAST, 200-300ms, ease-out, so the flow feels effortless, not cinematic-slow. The ONLY justified longer moment is the count-up reveal (1.5-2.5s) because it is the emotional payoff the user is watching on purpose. (3) Never gate a tap behind an animation finishing; let users tap through or skip the count-up. (4) Make key moments user-triggered (tap to scan -> count begins within 0.1s) so motion reads as cause-and-effect, not auto-playing decoration (Tuch 2009: animated interfaces cut comprehension 26% vs static; auto-animation annoys ~92%). (5) After the reveal, the number must REST as a still, calm figure, never loop or re-pulse, because perpetual motion around a health number reads as ALARM to an anxious heart-conscious audience. (6) MANDATORY reduce-motion fallback on every screen via @Environment(\\.accessibilityReduceMotion): if count-up is off, show the final Vitality Age immediately with a crossfade; replace orb pulsing and parallax with a still composition. The 35-50 anxious cohort statistically includes more vestibular sensitivity, so the meaning must never live ONLY in the motion. Respect the silent switch and haptics-off so the peak degrades gracefully to a purely visual landing."
