Social proof — where it goes in Laso

Three placements, ranked by evidence strength. The yellow dashed box is the new element on each screen. Everything else is the real screen as it ships today.

The "3x" claim is not in any mockup, on purpose. A health outcome number like "users improve 3x" is a legal establishment claim. FTC needs your own study to back it before it runs, a testimonial does not launder it, and "results may vary" does not cure it. Apple can also reject the app for it. So we show only what is honest today: real ratings, real counts, and the user's own history. Outcome multipliers wait until you own a cohort study.

What each proof is doing

  • ✓
    Home — first-party history. "You kept recovery green 5 of 7 days." Safest and strongest, because it is true for that user and no one can dispute it. Specificity beats a wall of generic 5-stars.
  • !
    Onboarding — real count / "people like you." Descriptive norm, the most robust social-proof effect (Cialdini). Use only your true user count; never round up to a plausible-looking number. A/B test it.
  • !
    Paywall — real rating + reviews. Highest-converting spot, but gate it behind genuinely having 50–100+ reviews, and show the real 4.2–4.7 value.

Never show: low counts, "0 reviews", or "most people quit" framing. Negative or tiny social proof actively reduces the behavior you want (Cialdini's Petrified Forest sign tripled theft). All proof copy routes through Remote Config so a non-compliant line can be pulled instantly without a release.
