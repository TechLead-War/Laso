Why it reads as premium

Seven decisions separate this from the generic ring-and-number island.

1 One signal, concentric

Compact shows a single glanceable signal that hugs the pill's curve. No clutter fighting the camera gap.

2 Time-of-day identity

Color and content shift with the body's day — calm green dawn, amber strain, indigo wind-down. The island is a clock for your physiology.

3 It breathes

A soft pulse glow behind the ring and a live ECG/orb tell you data is alive, not a frozen screenshot.

4 Earned color

True-black base per Apple; brand color used only where it carries meaning (the score arc, the action). Restraint reads expensive.

5 Expanded = a briefing

One insight sentence, three supporting stats with hairline dividers, one clear action. Glance, understand, act.

6 Honest staleness

After 20h the arc and number desaturate — your existing freshness rule, now visible. It never lies about old data.

7 Ends clean

Today's Score self-ends at local midnight; Breathwork at session end. Nothing lingers past its meaning.
