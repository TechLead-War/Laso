Laso · Screen 15 · Preview · FutureSelf, not a feature list

The old card was "By day 7 your Vitality Age moves" plus five day-by-day rows, a feature brag and a soft fast-change promise. The rethink turns the 7-day plan into the near end of a one-year line, then seals a message to your future self at the far end. Center is the pick.

  • Future self made real. Your own age on your own line, today bending toward one year out, makes "me next year" a continuous person, not a stranger, which is what cuts future-discounting. Hershfield 2011 future self-continuity
  • The note is the commitment. One tap to leave a line for next year is a textbook active written promise to yourself, the act of writing creates the follow-through pressure before any feature is used. Cialdini commitment, NN/g
  • It returns on a dated appointment. Showing the note back on this exact day in 2027 turns the promise into an implementation intention and the delayed-gratification payoff. Implementation intentions · episodic future thinking, Peters & Buchel
  • The 7-day plan, without a list. The first week is the near node on the line, so the plan is felt as the start of the journey, not five rows of feature copy. Progressive disclosure, one idea per screen
  • The high-note ending. A drawn line settling into a sealed envelope is the calm, hopeful close peak-end wants right before pricing, not a sales pitch. Peak-end rule, NN/g
  • Reduce Motion: line drawn full, all three nodes shown at once, envelope rests on the year mark, the meaning never lived only in the travel. Apple HIG Reduce Motion
