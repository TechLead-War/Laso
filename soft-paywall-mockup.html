The close: the same plan sheet you ship today, but it rises over their own blurred report and lists what is already written about them, not a feature list. The headline is what is waiting, not what Laso does.

Why this converts

Blur, do not hide

Locked cards keep their exact shape, color and motion. The user sees precisely what they are missing. A hidden feature list creates no desire.

It is addressed to them

The ring, the band pill and the half readable pattern lines are computed from their real HealthKit data. It reads like an unopened letter about their own body.

Loss grows daily

"9 patterns" becomes 12 tomorrow. The app keeps computing for free, so the debt of unread knowledge compounds every night they sleep.

One quiet close

No popups, no timers. Every screen does the selling, the single blue button only collects the decision. Check-ins and questions stay typeable so users keep investing.
