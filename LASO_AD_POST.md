# ONE THING — 15 second cut

## The idea

Every morning you guess whether to push or back off. Laso already knows. The
whole film is one shot of a phone, used twice, where only the screen changes.
That change is the story, so the message is on screen from frame one and there
is nothing to wait for.

No crowd, no dialogue, no voiceover, no music bed. Three quiet locked shots.

## Why the screen is blank in the generations

AI cannot draw the real Laso UI. So the phone is generated as a flat white
blank rectangle, which is a clean surface to track, and the real app screen is
composited on in post. That is how phone ads have always been made. It means
the product is legible, on brand, and actually ours.

## The cut

| Master | Source | What is on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00.0–00:02.2 | laso_01_insert | real Laso screen, low recovery |
| 00:02.2–00:06.0 | laso_02_stop | she reads it and stays in |
| 00:06.0–00:07.6 | laso_01_insert again | same shot, screen now shows the morning after |
| 00:07.6–00:11.5 | laso_03_go | she opens the door and goes into the light |
| 00:11.5–00:15.0 | end card | |

The repeat of the insert at 00:06.0 is what tells you a night has passed. No
date card, no dissolve, no explaining.

## Screen comps, real app copy

Screen one, 00:00.0–00:02.2, the hook. Full Laso card, cobalt on white:

> **NEXT UP · TODAY**
> HRV 38 ms, 14% below recent average.
> Take it easy and aim for an early night.

Screen two, 00:06.0–00:07.6, same framing, same phone position:

> **NEXT UP · TODAY**
> Your recovery is +12 higher this morning.

Both lines are shipped app copy, not ad copy. Track them to the phone with a
corner pin. The insert is locked off, so the track is four static corners plus
a soft screen reflection to sell the glass.

## Sound

Room tone under everything at a low fixed level. One soft breath at 00:04.
Two dry watch pulses on the frame the screen changes at 00:06.0. The door and
the street at 00:08. A warm two-note rising pad at 00:07.6 when the door opens,
and the same two notes as the end-card sting. One file, reused in every Laso
film so it compounds.

Nothing shouts. The loudest thing in the film is a door.

## End card

Full-bleed cobalt, LASO in white with a crimson underline, App Store badge.

> **Laso reads your night and tells you the one thing to do today.**
> LASO

## Clean-up

- Screen: if any take renders icons or glare on the white rectangle, patch it
  flat before compositing. The insert is locked off so one static patch holds.
- Watch: if the wrist watch renders a glow, matte it black.
- Grade: one LUT across all three. Keep the entryway cool and the doorway light
  warm, so the last shot is the only warm frame in the film.

## First thing to check on the take

The phone screen in laso_01_insert must come back genuinely blank and evenly
lit. That shot is the hook, the product and the payoff. If it has glare or
invented icons, re-roll it before looking at anything else.
