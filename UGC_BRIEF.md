# Laso — UGC Brief

Everything another agent needs to write UGC (user generated content) ad scripts for the app. All facts below come from the app's own code and website copy.

---

## 1. The app in one line

Laso is an iOS health app. It reads your Apple Health data (Apple Watch, plus Oura, Garmin, WHOOP through Apple Health), scores it, and every morning gives you one clear answer about what your body needs today, plus one thing to do about it. Everything is processed on your phone.

- Product name: **Laso**
- Platform: iOS 17+
- Website: lasohealth.fit
- App Store ID: 6762501313
- Instagram: @healthlaso
- Bundle: com.lasohealth.app

---

## 2. Core promise (what to sell in a UGC ad)

Primary tagline: **"Finally, understand your body."**

Supporting lines actually used by the brand:
- "Every morning, one clear answer about what your body needs today. Built from your data. Not an age group average."
- "Every morning, five numbers that actually mean something."
- "It tells you what to do, not just what happened."
- "Your Apple Watch already collects the data. Laso actually does something with it."
- "Your personal health guide, powered by Apple Health and your wearable."

The core insight to hammer in UGC: **most health apps compare you to a population average. Laso compares you to your own baseline** and turns raw data into a single daily readiness answer plus one concrete action.

---

## 3. The hero hook: Vitality Age

The single strongest hook, used in onboarding and marketing:

> "Your watch already knows how old your body really is. We read it in 30 seconds, then tell you the one thing to do next."

Vitality Age estimates how old your body actually is versus your real age, from your health data. The reveal moment is emotional: "You are [N] years younger than your real age of [X]" or "[N] years older." Then: "Now let's make it even younger."

This is the best UGC opener. The "am I older or younger than my age?" curiosity gap is proven to stop scrolls.

## 4. Main features (what the app actually shows)

- **Daily action card ("NEXT UP · TODAY")** — one concrete thing to do today, e.g. "a 10 minute wind down walk after dinner."
- **Health / Recovery Score (0 to 100)** — a daily orb with a "Why" breakdown. Day types: "Green Day. Push Hard." / "Yellow Day. Maintain." / "Red Day. Recover."
- **Vitality Age** — body age vs real age, 90 day trend, per metric contribution.
- **Strain** — how much physical effort your body handled today, with target ranges.
- **Stress Monitor** — pressure your body is under right now: Relaxed / Building / Real strain.
- **Sleep + Sleep Coach** — sleep debt, "Tonight's Goal / Bed by [time]", recommended sleep.
- **Insights / patterns** — personal correlations like "When you have 3+ cups of coffee, your [metric] drops X%. Based on N days of your data."
- **Health States timeline** — labels each day: Recovery, Peak Performance, Stressed, Under-Slept, Fatigued, Balanced.
- **Areas to Watch (early warnings)** — e.g. "Blood Pressure Trending Up", always tagged "for your information only, not medical advice."
- **Live vitals** — activity rings, live heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, last workout.
- **Cycle Tracking** — menstrual, follicular, ovulatory phases.
- **Weekly Review + Year in Review** — shareable reports, streaks.
- **Widgets + Live Activities** — readiness on home/lock screen, Dynamic Island wind-down countdown, breathwork guides.
- **Referral** — "Invite friends, earn free Pro."

Depth stat for credibility: **70+ health metrics** tracked.

## 5. Onboarding hooks (the emotional beats to reuse in UGC)

The app's 16 screen onboarding is basically a proven UGC script. Reuse these beats:

1. "Vitality Age. Your watch already knows how old your body really is."
2. Ask goals: Sleep better / Steady energy / Train smarter / Manage stress / Stay healthy long-term / Weight goal.
3. Ask what's bugging you: Tired mornings, Restless nights, Foggy thinking, Anxiety, Low motivation, Slow recovery, Mood swings.
4. Connect Apple Health: "Now Laso can read your story." / "Your data never leaves your phone."
5. Scan animation: "Laso is listening to your body. Reading [X] of history."
6. Heart reveal: "This is your heart, right now."
7. Sleep reveal: "You are [gap] short of where you want to be."
8. HRV reveal: "[Weekday]s land harder than the rest. Most people miss it. We won't."
9. Vitality Age reveal + "YOUR NEXT STEP" card targeting your weakest metric.
10. Paywall: "Your body is [N] years younger than your age. Keep it that way."
11. Done: "Your pathway starts now. Tonight brings your first step. Tomorrow morning we tell you if it worked."

Best UGC line to steal: **"Most people miss it. We won't."**

## 6. Target audience

- Apple Watch owners (also Oura, Garmin, WHOOP users, since data comes through Apple Health).
- Two overlapping groups:
  - **Longevity / general wellness** — people curious about aging well, "am I healthy?", Vitality Age.
  - **Athletes / optimizers** — recovery, strain targets, train-smart crowd (overlaps WHOOP and Oura buyers; the site has "vs WHOOP" and "vs Oura" pages).
- Pain points to speak to: tired mornings, bad sleep, stress, no idea what their watch data means, overwhelmed by numbers with no action.

---

## 7. Brand voice (match this in scripts)

- Warm, plain English, second person, calm. Anti-clinical.
- Renames medical jargon for normal people: HRV → "Heart Calm Signal", VO2 Max → "Cardio Fitness", REM → "Dream Sleep", AFib → "Irregular Heartbeat".
- Action oriented: always "here's what to do," never just data.
- Not alarming, not hype. Reassuring and personal.
- Privacy is a real selling point: **on-device, no account required, data stays on your phone.**

Never do: fear-mongering, fake medical claims, clinical tone. Laso is explicitly **not a medical device** and copy always avoids medical advice.

## 8. Pricing / offer for the CTA

- Free 7 day trial, then Pro (annual default, monthly option).
- Referral: free month of Pro for you and a friend.
- Trial framing used in app: Today → reminder 2 days before → renews. CTA: "Start free trial."

## 9. UGC angle ideas (starting points for the script agent)

1. **Vitality Age reveal** — "I found out my body is 6 years older than me. Here's how I'm fixing it." (curiosity + transformation)
2. **Watch guilt** — "Your Apple Watch has been collecting data for years and doing nothing with it."
3. **Morning number** — "Every morning this app tells me one thing to do. That's it. And it works."
4. **Anti-average** — "Every health app compares you to a 40 year old average. This one compares you to you."
5. **Sleep debt** — "I thought I slept fine. Then it showed me I'm 11 hours in debt."
6. **Stress reality check** — "It told me my body was stressed before I even felt it."
7. **Privacy** — "All of this runs on my phone. Nothing leaves the device."
8. **Recovery day types** — "Green day, push hard. Red day, recover. Finally simple."

---

## 10. Must-follow constraints for scripts

- No medical claims or cure/diagnosis language. Wellness and information only.
- Keep it plain English, no clinical jargon (use the friendly metric names above).
- Always pair a "what happened" with a "what to do."
- Privacy line is a bonus, not the hero, unless the angle is specifically privacy.
- CTA: 7 day free trial, download on the App Store.
