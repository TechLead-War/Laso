The Dynamic Island, drawn to its real regions.

The first mockup was a freeform card the island can't reproduce — its top splits into narrow slots around the camera, which truncated "Readiness" to "Re...". This version models the four real regions from researched specs, so the layout you review is exactly what ships. The rule that drives it: leading and trailing and center are narrow; only the bottom is roomy — so every wide element (label, insight, stats, action) lives in the bottom card, and the narrow top slots hold only a glyph and the time.

Why this is buildable 1:1. In SwiftUI it maps straight to the API: .leading → the sunrise glyph, .trailing → the time, .center → empty, .bottom → the whole card (ring + Readiness pill + insight + 3‑stat row, then the action + weak‑pillar chip). Because the wide content sits only in .bottom (full island width, clear of the camera), nothing truncates, and the bottom takes the full height under the camera so the action never clips.

Honest gaps (flagged, not hidden): the TrueDepth cutout's exact point size and vertical offset are not published by Apple anywhere — the cutout here is a centered approximation. The expanded max height is reported as 144 pt by one source and 160 pt by another; this design targets ~150 pt to stay safely under both. The ~371 pt width and 84/160 pt heights come from one dev measurement (UX Planet), not an Apple page. So treat the pixel proportions as close, not exact — but the region model (narrow top, roomy bottom) is solid and is what makes the layout correct.
