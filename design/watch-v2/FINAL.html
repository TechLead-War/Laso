Laso Watch — the final design

One word telling you what your body is doing, the reason under it, and how much room you have left today. Chosen on the wrist from the wrist's own sensors, so it is never stale and never sends you to your phone. Every screen of the shipping design is on this page.

46mm · 208 × 248 pt Dark only Alex, 34 · Tuesday 14:32 Readiness 62 · fair Dashed outline = a Laso surface
The rule this design is built on: a word only reaches the headline if the user could not already know it. That is why STAND, DRINK, WALK and COAST are not here — Apple's own rings and stand reminders already say those, and the wrist has exactly one line to spend.

01The app — three pages, Digital Crown moves between them

Page 1 is the answer. Page 2 is why. Page 3 is what is coming. Nothing is more than one crown turn from the answer, and the readiness number is deliberately on page 2, not page 1.

02The seven words

Four are orders and fire rarely, only when Laso knows something invisible to the user. Three are states and cover most days — a fact measured against this user's own baseline, never a command.

03The watch face — where most of the value lands

Apple states people may never explicitly launch a watch app. So the complication is the product and the app is the detail view. Each family gets its own deep link, never a shared one.

04Smart Stack — crown down from the face

Only accessoryRectangular and accessoryCircular reach the Smart Stack. The card carries the answer plus one button, so the common case never opens the app at all.

05Notifications — two a day maximum, fired on change

Notifications buy an open now, not a habit: a 3.5× lift in the next hour but no measurable effect on long-term retention. So they fire on a change against a rolling average, never on an absolute threshold, and every one can be hushed for the day.

06The states — including the ones that usually get skipped

There is no state where the wrist has nothing true to say. "Open Laso on your iPhone" does not exist in this design. In Always-On the health values are genuinely hidden, not just dimmed, because anyone standing behind you can read a dimmed screen.
